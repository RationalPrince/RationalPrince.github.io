About

An independent research desk for self-directed investors.

RationalPrince publishes institutional-style research on energy, maritime chokepoints and sovereign capital, and builds the tools individuals need to apply it to their own money.

What we do

Large funds employ teams to read filings, track physical flows and connect geopolitics to price. Most individual investors get headlines instead. RationalPrince exists to close that gap: each product explains how a professional desk frames a market, what it watches, and what would prove the thesis wrong.

The research covers three areas that move markets before the news catches up: energy markets and their structure, chokepoints such as the Strait of Hormuz, and the behaviour of sovereign capital and defense spending. Alongside it sit practical desk tools, including an AI research system, a portfolio tracker, a financial planner and a Notion research dashboard.

How the work is made

Products are written to be read once and used repeatedly. They favour frameworks, scenarios and checklists over predictions, state their assumptions plainly, and cite sources where figures matter. AI tools are used to speed up research and drafting; every product teaches readers to verify output before acting on it, and we hold our own work to the same standard.

How we sell

Everything is sold outright, at a one-time price, through Gumroad, with some titles also on the Stan store. There is no subscription, no signal group and no advisory relationship. The Sovereign Brief is free, so you can judge the work before paying for anything.

What we are not

RationalPrince is not a registered investment adviser or broker-dealer. Nothing we publish is a recommendation to buy or sell any security, and nothing is tailored to your circumstances. Use the research as an input to your own decisions.
