Privacy

Privacy policy.

This page explains what information is collected when you visit rationalprince.com or buy a RationalPrince product, and what you can do about it.

What this site collects

rationalprince.com is a static website. It has no account system and no forms, and it does not ask for your name, email address or payment details. The site itself does not store personal information.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how the site is used: which pages are viewed, which products are clicked, roughly where visitors come from, and what device and browser they use. Google Analytics sets cookies and receives your IP address to produce these reports. We use the reports only to improve the site and the products. You can block analytics with your browser's privacy settings, a content blocker, or Google's opt-out add-on.

Purchases

Checkout is handled by Gumroad, which opens on this site or on gumroad.com. When you buy, Gumroad collects the details needed to process payment and deliver your files, such as your email address and payment information. Card details go to Gumroad and its payment processors; RationalPrince never sees or stores them. As the seller, we receive order information from Gumroad such as your email address and what you bought, and we use it only to deliver and support your purchase. Gumroad's handling of your data is described in its privacy policy. Titles sold through the Stan store are covered by Stan's own privacy policy, available on its checkout pages.

Third-party content

Pages load fonts from Google Fonts and product images from Gumroad's file servers, so those services receive your IP address when the page loads. Links to social networks take you to sites with their own privacy policies.

Email

If you download the free Sovereign Brief, your email address is collected by Gumroad as part of that order. We may use it to send research updates; every message includes a way to unsubscribe, and we do not sell or share email lists.

Your choices

You can ask what order information we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, by sending a direct message through any channel on the Contact page. Requests about payment data should go to Gumroad directly.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
