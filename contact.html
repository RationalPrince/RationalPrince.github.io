Help with an order

Every purchase is processed by Gumroad and delivered by email straight after checkout. Your files also stay in your Gumroad library, so you can download them again at any time by signing in to Gumroad with the email you used to buy.

If something is missing or a file won't open, send a direct message on X or LinkedIn with the product name and your Gumroad receipt number. Please don't share card details in a message; we never need them.

Common order questions

I didn't get my download email. Check spam and promotions folders first, then sign in to Gumroad with the email you used at checkout. Every purchase sits in your Gumroad library.

Can I get a different file format? Most products ship as PDF; the Portfolio Tracker is a Google Sheet and the Macro Research Dashboard is a Notion template. Message us if you need help opening either.

Where is the free research? Explainers on oil markets and sovereign capital are on the research page.
